THE BUCKET LIST ROUND
⛳ THE OLD COURSE — 600 Years of Golf History

Golf has been played here since the 15th century. The Swilcan Bridge. Hell Bunker. The Road Hole. The Valley of Sin. Every golfer alive dreams of this round. Today you live the dream.

Morning — Free time
🌅 West Sands Beach Walk
Made famous in Chariots of Fire. Walk the 2km beach, look back at the town. An iconic St Andrews morning ritual before the big round.
12:00pm — Warm Up
🏌️ Practice Range + R&A Museum Visit
Hit balls on the Himalayas putting green (public, free!) near the 1st tee. Pop into the R&A World Golf Museum — 500+ years of history next to the course. Photo outside the R&A Clubhouse is mandatory.
1:00pm → ~5:30pm
⛳ Play the Old Course
Key holes:
14th — Hell Bunker (AVOID)
17th — Road Hole (survive!)
18th — Cross the Swilcan Bridge SLOWLY. Group photo. Every one of you.
Hire a caddie! Old Course caddies are legends — their local knowledge is worth every penny (£60 + tip). They know every bump, break, and story.
Evening — Celebrate!
🍾 Celebration Dinner
Road Hole Restaurant (Old Course Hotel) — 3 AA Rosettes, overlooking the 17th hole. The ultimate post-round dinner.
Then: The Jigger Inn for a dram and the retelling of every shot, badly.

😤Carnoustie Championship
TEE OFF: 11:30am · "Car-nasty"
11:00am — Drive 35 min
🚗 Head to Carnoustie, Angus
Links Parade, Carnoustie DD7 7JE. Home of 8 Open Championships. The Barry Burn awaits. Arrive early to warm up — you'll need it.
11:30am → ~4:30pm
⛳ Play Carnoustie Championship Course
Nicknamed "Car-nasty" by the pros. The Barry Burn winds through the course. Famously ruined Jean Van de Velde's 1999 Open victory on the 18th. Ben Hogan called it "a great and difficult course." You'll agree.
⛳ Survival tip: STAY OUT of the Barry Burn on 17 and 18. Play conservatively. Making par on those holes is a victory.
Evening
🥩 Balgove Larder Steak Barn
Farm-to-table on outskirts of St Andrews. Own butchery. Incredible steaks! Rustic converted barn. After Carnoustie, you deserve a great steak.

The Dram Trail
Whisky distilleries worth the detour
🥃 Kingsbarns Distillery
📍 East Newhall Farm, Kingsbarns · 5 min from Kingsbarns Golf
Award-winning single malt in a beautiful Georgian farmstead. First cask 2015. Fruity, floral, exceptional. Also: Darnley's Gin Cottage next door.
💡 Tour + 3 drams £25pp. Book ahead. Open daily.
🥃 Eden Mill Distillery
📍 Guardbridge, St Andrews · 10 min drive
Stunning new visitor centre on the Eden Estuary. The Lookout Cocktail Bar on top floor has incredible views to St Andrews. Gin + whisky experiences.
💡 Gin tour £15pp. Also has a golf simulator! Lookout Bar — book a table for sunset cocktails.
🥃 Lindores Abbey Distillery
📍 Newburgh, Fife · 45 min from St Andrews
Scotland's FIRST written record of Scotch whisky was here in 1494! Medieval abbey ruins. A genuine pilgrimage for whisky lovers.
💡 Tours 10am, 12:30pm, 2:30pm. Book ahead. A worthy rest-day detour.
🥃 Johnnie Walker Princes Street
📍 145 Princes Street, Edinburgh · In the city centre
World's greatest whisky experience. 8 floors, immersive, all 5 Scotch regions, rooftop bar views over Edinburgh. 3 personalised cocktails.
💡 From £35pp. Book essential — sells out. Do this on Edinburgh days (17–19 Jul).
